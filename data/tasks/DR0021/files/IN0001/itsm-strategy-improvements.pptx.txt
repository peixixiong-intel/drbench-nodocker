Introduction In light of our expansion, we must revamp our ITSM strategies to address incident, problem, and change management more efficiently. This presentation will explore potential improvements in IT governance, workflow automation, and system integration.

Slide 3:
Current IT Governance Structure Our Q3 2024 ITSM tracker has revealed a 22% surge in average incident resolution time, highlighting a crucial area for improvement. An identified cause for this increase is the disarticulated IT systems being used by our regional stores, causing inconsistencies and inefficiencies in incident resolution. Lack of standardized processes for incident, problem, and change management across the regions has further compounded the issue. Communication challenges within regional teams have led to inconsistencies in incident management, contributing to escalating resolution times. The fragmentation of our IT systems and processes across regional stores has not only affected incident resolution but also problem and change management. The current scenario underscores the need for a more integrated, standardized IT governance structure to enhance our IT service management. Potential solutions could include IT system integration, automation of workflow, and improvements in governance as evidenced by Thirdera’s success in the retail industry.
