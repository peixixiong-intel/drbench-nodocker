Slide 2:
Introduction As we consider a strategic shift from dealership franchising to direct-to-customer, it's crucial we understand potential benefits and key steps to drive sales and higher margins. This presentation will provide insights on how this transition could shape Elexion's future in 2027.

Slide 3:
Current State of Elexion's Sales Operations Our sales process currently involves a 42-day journey from initial customer contact to final sale closure. This period, as recorded in January 2025, is representative of the average time it takes for our dealership network to finalize a sale. Our sales operations through the dealer network require this considerable time investment, potentially impacting customer satisfaction and sales efficiency. A shift to a direct sales model could potentially reduce this period, streamlining our sales process and enhancing customer experience. Considering the reported extra costs added by dealerships, a direct model could also provide financial advantages by eliminating middleman markups. This strategic shift might provide us with a competitive edge, especially considering the significant annual revenues of top dealership groups in America.
