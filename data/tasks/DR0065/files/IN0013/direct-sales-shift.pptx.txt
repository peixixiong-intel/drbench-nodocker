Current State of Franchise Dealerships Dealerships have been identified as adding significant markups to the manufacturer's suggested retail price, with additional fees potentially reaching up to $20,000 per vehicle. The traditional dealership model has led to annual revenues of around $100 billion for the top 10 dealership groups in America. In contrast, Elexion's 2024 market study highlighted the effectiveness of online engagement, demonstrating higher lead conversion rates for customers using an online configurator than in-person dealership visits. The digital-first approach to sales could reduce overhead costs related to dealership maintenance and operations. The shift to an online model could also mitigate the unnecessary markups and fees associated with traditional dealerships, potentially boosting margins. This could also create an opportunity for Elexion to redefine its sales strategies by leveraging data-driven insights from online customer interactions.
