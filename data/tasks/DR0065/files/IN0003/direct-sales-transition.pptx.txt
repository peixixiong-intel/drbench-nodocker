Introduction As we consider shifting our sales model from dealership franchising to a direct-to-customer approach, it's crucial to understand the potential benefits and how to leverage them. This presentation will explore strategies for driving sales and higher margins through this transition.

Slide 3:
Understanding the Direct-to-Customer Model In 2024, our vehicle distribution strategy resulted in an average dealer cost of $38,000 per vehicle. The same vehicles were then sold to customers at an average price point of $44,275. The difference of $6,275 per unit represents the margins currently captured by dealers. Shifting to a direct sales model could potentially redirect these margins to Elexion Automotive. The direct-to-customer approach could eliminate unnecessary dealership markup, increasing our margins and reducing costs for the consumer. This model could strengthen customer relationships and brand loyalty, driving sales growth. The direct sales model could make Elexion more competitive, given the massive revenues of top dealership groups.

Impact of Sales Model Change on Profit Margins In many industries, a direct-to-customer sales model can lead to an increase in profit margins The model can significantly reduce the cost of intermediaries It can also lead to a decrease in the cost of inventory management However, the model may require significant investment in IT infrastructure It may also require the company to take on the cost of logistics and delivery The model can lead to improved cash flow due to faster payment cycles Lastly, the model may lead to an increase in customer loyalty due to personalized service
