Introduction As we continue to explore innovative sales approaches, we are considering the potential of a direct-to-customer model. This presentation will examine the potential benefits and implications of this shift on our sales and margins.

Slide 3:
Understanding Electric Vehicle Market Trends Observations from Q1 2025 show a notable increase in sales among our primary competitors who have adopted the direct-to-customer selling model. This increase can be attributed to the elimination of dealership markups and fees, which have been reported to reach up to $20,000 per vehicle. The direct-to-customer strategy not only increases margins but also provides the opportunity to control the end-to-end customer experience. Considering the substantial revenues of dealership groups, transitioning to a direct sales model could potentially redirect a significant portion of those revenues to manufacturers. A well-executed transition could position Elexion as a leader in the EV market by combining our commitment to sustainability and user-centric technology with a more customer-friendly sales model. It's crucial to develop a robust and efficient sales system capable of handling the complexities of direct customer interactions. Careful monitoring and analysis of competitor performance can provide valuable insights and help in refining our sales strategy.

Transitioning to Direct-to-Customer Sales: Prospects and Challenges Traditional automotive retail model involves franchised dealerships which act as intermediaries between automakers and consumers Direct-to-consumer model eliminates the need for dealership intermediaries, potentially leading to cost savings Challenges may include regulatory hurdles, logistical issues, and customer service concerns Direct sales model could offer greater control over the customer experience However, transitioning to this model may require significant investments in IT infrastructure Disintermediation also poses risks, such as increased responsibility for customer service and after-sales care

Slide 5:
Potential Impact on Elexion's Sales and Margins Moving to a direct sales model could potentially increase Elexion's gross margins by eliminating dealer markups It could also improve Elexion's control over pricing and inventory However, the transition could also present significant up-front costs, potentially impacting short-term profitability Direct sales could improve customer experience and brand perception, potentially driving long-term sales growth Elexion would also need to manage after-sales service, which could affect margins Elexion's financial performance will depend on various factors, including the successful implementation of the direct sales model, market conditions, and regulatory environment

Slide 6:
Conclusion In conclusion, transitioning to a direct-to-customer sales model could potentially drive higher sales and margins. Careful planning, extensive market research, and cross-functional collaboration will be critical for a successful shift.
