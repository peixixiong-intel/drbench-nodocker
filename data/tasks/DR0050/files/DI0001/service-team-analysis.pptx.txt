Customer Service Team Composition Team consists of a mix of full-time and part-time employees In 2023, a total of 50% of the customer service team was comprised of part-timers Majority of the part-time employees work during peak hours The team is overseen by a customer service manager with over 10 years of industry experience Training programs are regularly conducted to improve service quality The team is divided into sub-teams, each handling specific areas of customer service The team collaborates with other departments to resolve complex customer issues

Slide 4:
Customer Service Representatives Headcount In 2022, the customer service team grew by 20% As of January 2024, there were 22 full-time customer service representatives The company plans to hire more representatives in the next fiscal year The turnover rate in the customer service department is around 10% annually Employee retention strategies have been implemented to reduce turnover The company maintains a reserve list of potential hires to fill vacancies quickly

Slide 5:
Workload Distribution Across Customer Service Team Workload is distributed based on the complexity of customer issues Peak hours workload is managed by a higher number of part-time representatives Each representative handles an average of 150 customer interactions per week The company uses a CRM tool to track and distribute workload Annual leave and sick days are factored into workload distribution Regular meetings are held to assess workload and make necessary adjustments The company is looking into AI-based solutions to manage workload efficiently

Conclusion The current size of our customer service team aligns with our operational needs, ensuring efficient and high-quality service to our customers. Moving forward, we will continuously monitor and adjust our team size and composition to optimally respond to customer needs.
