Introduction In our continuous efforts to improve customer satisfaction, we will explore the ways in which enhancing patient communication can reduce service calls. Our focus is on the implementation of effective strategies for the first quarter of 2025.

Current State of Customer Service Calls Post-consultation, patients receive automated emails containing the date of appointment and the provider's name Comprehensive visit summary, including billing details and follow-up directives, is delivered manually 24-48 hours post-consultation Absence of immediate, detailed follow-up information could be contributing to increased service calls No updates or changes to this process are currently planned for the year 2025 Implementation of AI-based patient communication technology could potentially reduce customer service load There is a need to explore options for enhancing patient communication, such as AI or automated follow-up reminders Despite excitement in the healthcare industry, only 13% of healthcare group leaders plan to focus on AI in the next 12 months

Slide 4:
The Impact of Patient Communication on Service Calls Effective patient communication can potentially reduce the number of service calls In a 2023 industry report, 40% of healthcare businesses reported fewer service calls after improving patient communication The same report indicated a direct correlation between improved patient communication and increased patient satisfaction Advanced communication technologies can automate routine patient interactions, reducing the workload on customer service Patients with clear understanding of their health status and treatment plans tend to make fewer service calls More transparent and real-time communication can increase patient trust, indirectly reducing service calls

Slide 5:
Strategic Approaches to Improve Patient Communication Implementing a robust CRM system can help manage patient interactions and improve communication Training customer service representatives on empathetic communication can enhance patient interaction Regular patient surveys can provide valuable insights into areas of communication that need improvement Offering multichannel communication options such as email, chat, and phone can cater to patient preferences Utilizing patient personas can help tailor communication strategies to different patient groups A clear and comprehensive FAQ section on the website can preemptively answer patient queries, reducing service calls

Slide 6:
Conclusion By improving patient communication, we can significantly reduce service calls and enhance customer satisfaction. Let's work together to implement these strategies and measure their effectiveness in Q12025.
