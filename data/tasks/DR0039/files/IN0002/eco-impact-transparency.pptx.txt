Introduction As part of our ongoing commitment to sustainability, we are closely monitoring carbon footprint transparency in our industry. This presentation will analyze the communication methods and impacts on customer perception among online grocery retailers in North America.

Slide 3:
Carbon Footprint Metrics Among Competitors The 2024 sustainability report showed a clear regional difference in customer perception of our green initiatives. In the Canadian markets, our transparency on carbon footprint had a positive impact on 89% of our customers. This contrasts with our U.S. markets, where the positive impact was reported by 71% of customers. This demonstrates the importance of environmental transparency in maintaining customer trust and loyalty. The regional difference in perception suggests a need for region-specific communication and engagement strategies. It's important to note that our competitors, like Walmart, have also embarked on significant sustainability efforts such as the 'Gigaton Project'. To stay competitive, we need to continually evaluate and communicate our carbon footprint metrics openly.
