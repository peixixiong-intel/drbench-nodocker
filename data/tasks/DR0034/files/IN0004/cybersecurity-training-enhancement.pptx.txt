Slide 3:
Current Cybersecurity Threat Landscape in Retail With daily processing of thousands of transactions, digital POS and e-commerce platforms at Lee's Market are under constant cyber threat. Approximately 65% of reported cyberattacks in the retail sector target these critical business systems. Given the high risk, it is crucial to prioritize employee training on POS and e-commerce platform security in the first three quarters of 2026. The industry has witnessed 80% of retailers being victims of cyberattacks in the previous year, emphasizing the urgent need for a robust cybersecurity training program. Lee's Market's brand reputation and operational efficiency hinge on the effectiveness of our cybersecurity measures; 53% of retailers have experienced reputational damage following security breaches. Furthermore, 68% of retailers reported operational downtime as the most common consequence of cyberattacks, directly affecting customer trust and revenue streams. It is therefore imperative that our cybersecurity training and incident response processes be designed to mitigate these threats and minimize potential damage.
