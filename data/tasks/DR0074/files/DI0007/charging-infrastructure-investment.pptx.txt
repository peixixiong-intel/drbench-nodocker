EV Market Trends and Charging Infrastructure The global EV market is projected to grow at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 26% through 2026 In 2024, the total number of EV charging stations in the US exceeded 100,000 Public charging infrastructure remains a challenge, with less than one public charger for every ten EVs on the road in 2024 The North American EV market is expected to reach $180 billion by 2027 In 2023, the US government approved a $7.5 billion investment in EV charging infrastructure Elexion's home charging solution, Elexion Charge, was launched in 2019

Elexion's 2024 Charging Infrastructure Investments Elexion’s ‘Charge Ahead’ program aimed to accelerate the deployment of charging infrastructure In Q2 2024, Elexion expanded its charging network to 3 new states The company increased its charging stations by 30% in 2024 Elexion’s R&amp;D team developed a new fast-charging technology in 2024 The 'Charge Ahead' program was primarily funded through a mix of private investments and public grants

Partnerships and Collaborations for Charging Infrastructure Elexion partnered with GridPower in 2023 to enhance its charging technology In 2024, Elexion collaborated with CityCharge to install EV charging stations in urban areas Elexion joined the Charge America coalition to advocate for more investment in charging infrastructure The collaboration with state governments assisted in the expansion of Elexion’s charging network Discussions with European charging manufacturer, LightningCharge, began in late 2024
