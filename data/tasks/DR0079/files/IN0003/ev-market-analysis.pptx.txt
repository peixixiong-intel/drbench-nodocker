EV Adoption Rates and Market Trends China's dominance in EV production and battery cost efficiency, producing over 60% of global electric cars in 2023, is reshaping the competitive landscape, prompting North American automakers to reconsider their strategies. Major automakers like Tesla have responded by significantly reducing EV prices; Model Y saw a cut of roughly $15,000 between mid-2022 and early 2024. Such aggressive pricing strategies are shifting market positioning and could influence consumer adoption rates in North America ahead of 2026. Elexion faced operational challenges in Q3 2024, with 17% of mid-range EV orders experiencing at least a two-week delay due to supply chain issues in battery components. These delivery setbacks impacted Elexion's commitment to customers and could potentially affect consumer confidence and market share. Elexion's supply chain vulnerabilities reveal the need for a robust and responsive strategy to mitigate potential disruptions and maintain competitive positioning. Continued monitoring of competitive dynamics, pricing trends, and operational challenges is crucial to inform Elexion's strategic direction in the evolving North American EV market.

Impact of Regulatory Changes on EV Market The Biden administration announced a goal for 50% of new vehicle sales to be electric by 2030 In 2023, Canada increased its EV incentive program to encourage adoption California plans to ban the sale of new gasoline-powered vehicles by 2035 The introduction of stricter fuel economy standards in the US has boosted the EV market The phasing out of federal tax credits for EVs could impact future adoption rates The development of a unified charging standard in North America could drive EV adoption
