Market Dynamics: Electric Vehicle Adoption Trends In the first quarter of 2024, a significant number of our mid-income family customer segment, equivalent to 42%, were influenced by competitor pricing and features when considering alternate EV brands. This customer feedback reveals an emerging shift in the competitive landscape of the EV market, impacting our market positioning. Competitive pricing strategies, as evidenced by the reduction in Tesla’s Model Y pricing by approximately $15,000 between mid-2022 and early 2024, are actively reshaping customer expectations and purchasing behavior. China's dominance in the EV market, producing over 60% of global electric cars in 2023, has intensified global competition and is pressuring automakers, including Elexion, to adjust their strategies. Our cross-functional teams need to consider these market dynamics in order to make informed business decisions and maintain competitiveness. It is critical for Elexion to align its pricing and feature offerings with market trends to prevent further customer attrition to competitors. Leveraging our customer feedback, we need to refine our strategies to better meet customer expectations and preferences in the evolving EV market.

Competitor Strategies: Disruption in the EV Market In 2023, Tesla launched its Cybertruck, a revolutionary design that diverged from traditional pickup trucks. General Motors announced in 2020 its commitment to launch 30 new EVs worldwide by 2025. Many traditional automakers have formed strategic partnerships with tech companies to develop autonomous EVs. Ford's Mustang Mach-E, launched in late 2020, was the company's first all-electric SUV and marked a significant shift in its EV strategy. Some competitors are focusing on producing EVs for niche markets, such as luxury vehicles or commercial fleets. Elexion's main competitors have invested heavily in R&amp;D, with an average increase of 20% in their R&amp;D budgets from 2020 to 2023.

In 2023, Elexion increased its R&amp;D budget by 30% to develop innovative EV technologies.
