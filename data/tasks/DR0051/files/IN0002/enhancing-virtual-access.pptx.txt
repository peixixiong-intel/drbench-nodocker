Slide 3:
Current State: Patient Access and Satisfaction In 2024, we experienced a 42% rate of incomplete booking attempts on our platform. A significant 68% of these incomplete bookings were abandoned during the payment information input phase. The remaining 32% of users ceased their booking during the scheduling phase. Post-booking surveys indicated that a concerning 61% of these patients did not pursue alternate booking methods. A deeper analysis of these figures indicates a potential barrier at the payment and scheduling stages of our platform. The high rate of booking abandonments suggests an opportunity to streamline the user experience and improve patient access. Addressing these issues could lead to improved patient satisfaction and retention.
