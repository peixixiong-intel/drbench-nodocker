Overview of Virtual Consultation Process The process begins with the patient's request for a virtual consultation certificate MediConn's team verifies the patient’s details and eligibility A licensed healthcare professional is then assigned to the patient The consultation is scheduled based on the professional’s availability After the consultation, the professional generates a report Finally, the report is sent to the patient along with the certificate
