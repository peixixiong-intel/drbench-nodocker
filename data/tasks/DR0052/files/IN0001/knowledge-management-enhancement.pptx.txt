Slide 3:
Analyzing Current Knowledge Management Challenges MediConn's knowledge management system will be enhanced to adjust to the increasing volume of medical information, as seen in the 55 new medications approved in 2023. Effective January 1, 2026, a new feature will be implemented to aid in the prevention of knowledge-related errors. The new feature will ensure real-time alerts are delivered to our virtual care teams whenever clinical guidelines are updated. This real-time alert system, as outlined in the internal report published on October 13, 2025, will aid in reducing instances of medical errors. The system will ensure we stay ahead of potential knowledge gaps, and will prove instrumental in efficiently managing the growing complexity of medical information. By Q2 2026, we expect all virtual care teams to be fully integrated with this real-time alert system, supporting evidence-based practices.
