Slide 3:
Overview of Community Partnership Programs Lee's Market engaged in the sponsorship of three major Asian cultural festivals in 2022, a strategic approach to community engagement. The total investment for these sponsorships was $150,000, indicating our commitment to supporting culturally relevant events. The sponsorships resulted in significant media coverage, translating to an impressive 300% return on investment. Beyond financial gains, these sponsorships also led to a surge in new customer acquisition, with a 25% increase observed. It's noteworthy that these new customers were culturally relevant, aligning well with our core business of Asian groceries. The success of these sponsorships indicates the potential of community partnerships as a vehicle for brand promotion and customer acquisition. Given the results, it's recommended to continue exploring community partnerships, particularly those that align with our target demographic.
