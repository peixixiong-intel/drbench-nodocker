Battery Technology Advancements and Implications Elexion's R&amp;D tracking indicates that as of Q1 2026, 30% of our EV prototypes are powered by emerging battery technologies Specifically, a significant 22% of these prototypes are utilizing solid-state batteries, a technology that enhances energy density and reduces costs Additionally, 8% of our prototypes are equipped with sodium-ion batteries, a promising alternative contributing to diversification in battery technology Despite these advancements, lithium-ion batteries maintain a strong presence, powering 70% of our current prototypes These shifts in battery technology are in line with global trends as highlighted in the 2024 iea.org report, suggesting a strategic alignment with market demands As battery manufacturing expands outside China, our adoption of these emerging technologies could enhance our supply resilience However, the rising demand for minerals essential for battery production could potentially create supply bottlenecks, impacting our EV production and delivery timelines by 2026
