Slide 3:
Tele-health Industry Trends and Implications Our recent market research from 2023 indicates discomfort with virtual visits among 25% of our partnering hospitals' health professionals. This discomfort is mainly due to a lack of technological familiarity, causing a 30% increase in the setup time for virtual appointments. Considering the potential for over 50 million more in-person visits to be converted to virtual ones, as per the McKinsey report, we need to address this issue to capitalize on this trend. The reluctance of health professionals to adopt virtual visits could impact patient trust, satisfaction, and retention if not addressed. A focused training program for these health professionals could help to alleviate tech-related discomfort, streamline the setup process, and improve patient experience. Addressing this discomfort could also contribute to reduced labor costs, aligning with the industry trend of telehealth reducing labor costs by over 50%. Our goal, in line with industry trends and our strategic initiative, is to improve patient experience and thereby increase trust and retention by Q4 2026.

Current Patient Experience Assessment at MediConn MediConn Solutions served approximately 50,000 patients in Q2 2021. The average waiting time for consultations on our platform is 10 minutes. Our patient satisfaction score for Q3 2021 was an 8.2/10. In 2021, 20% of our patients used our services for mental health consultations. Our patient retention rate for Q4 2021 was 65%. As of Q3 2021, MediConn's mobile app had a 4-star rating in the Google Play Store. In Q4 2021, we experienced a 5% decrease in patient complaints compared to Q3.
