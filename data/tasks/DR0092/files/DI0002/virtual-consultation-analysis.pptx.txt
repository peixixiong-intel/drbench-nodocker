Overview of Virtual Patient Consultations MediConn Solutions has been providing virtual patient consultations since 2018 The company uses a combination of video conferencing and secure messaging for consultations All consultants are licensed healthcare professionals with specialized training in telehealth MediConn's proprietary software ensures HIPAA compliance during all consultations The company has seen a significant increase in virtual consultations since the COVID-19 pandemic MediConn Solutions currently provides the service to over 2000 patients

Average Duration of Consultations in FY2025 MediConn Solutions has been recording consultation duration data since 2018 The company uses this data to inform staffing and scheduling decisions However, the average consultation duration varies significantly based on the patient's health condition The company also monitors the time spent on post-consultation paperwork and follow-up tasks MediConn Solutions is continually seeking ways to improve the efficiency of virtual consultations The company uses machine learning algorithms to predict future consultation durations

Slide 5:
Implications and Opportunities for Future Efficiency Improving efficiency in virtual consultations is a key strategic objective for MediConn Solutions The company is investing in AI and machine learning technologies to enhance consultation efficiency MediConn Solutions is also exploring partnerships with other telehealth providers to share best practices Efficient consultations can help reduce patient waiting times and improve patient satisfaction MediConn Solutions has set a goal to reduce consultation duration by 10% over the next five years The company is also considering implementing a patient self-service portal to streamline pre-consultation processes
