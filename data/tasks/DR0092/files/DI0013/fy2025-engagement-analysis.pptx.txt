FY2025 Employee Engagement Analysis

Slide 2:
Introduction This presentation analyzes the results of the FY2025 employee engagement survey. We will focus on staff satisfaction index and its impact on MediConn's operations.

Slide 3:
Overview of FY2025 Employee Engagement Survey The survey was launched in Q1 FY2025 and remained open for a month Over 80% of MediConn employees participated in the survey The survey consisted of 20 questions covering various aspects of work life The survey used a Likert scale to measure responses Results were analysed by an independent third party to ensure unbiased reporting The survey results were compared to industry benchmarks to assess MediConn's performance The data was segmented by department, role, and tenure to identify specific areas of improvement

Slide 4:
Breakdown of Staff Satisfaction Index The Staff Satisfaction Index (SSI) covers multiple dimensions including work-life balance, recognition, and career growth Each dimension is given equal weight in the overall SSI calculation SSI scores can range from 0 (extremely dissatisfied) to 100 (extremely satisfied) MediConn's SSI has seen an upward trend over the last five years The SSI is a leading indicator of employee retention and productivity The SSI is monitored on a quarterly basis by the HR department

Slide 5:
Implications of Engagement Survey Results High staff satisfaction scores are correlated with lower turnover rates Survey results will guide the development of new HR policies and programs Departments with lower satisfaction scores will receive targeted interventions Senior leadership is committed to enhancing staff satisfaction based on survey results Continuous monitoring and improvement of staff satisfaction is a key strategic priority for MediConn The next survey will incorporate any changes made as a result of the FY2025 findings

Slide 6:
Conclusion The results of the FY2025 engagement survey indicate areas of strength and opportunities for improvement in staff satisfaction. We will use these insights to formulate strategic initiatives aimed at enhancing employee engagement and productivity.
