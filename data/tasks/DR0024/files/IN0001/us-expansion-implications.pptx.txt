Introduction As MediConn Solutions considers expanding its cash-only services into United States, we need to understand the regulatory landscape. This presentation will discuss potential challenges and the impact on our compliance strategies.

Slide 3:
Overview of U.S Healthcare Regulatory Landscape The U.S healthcare landscape is complex with diverse state-specific patient data privacy laws. Our compliance team has envisaged a multi-year strategy to understand, interpret, and implement these laws across all states. Taking into account the intricacies of these regulations, our team predicts a timeline extending to Q1 2027 for complete compliance. This estimate is based on an aggressive yet realistic approach in terms of resource allocation and project management. This timeline is critical as it directly impacts our expansion strategy and potential launch dates in different states. It implies that the roll-out of cash-only services in the U.S will need to be phased, rather than simultaneous across all states. The board should contemplate this timeline when discussing expansion and potential partnerships in the U.S.

Compliance Challenges of Cash-only Services Cash-only services can simplify billing and administrative processes It eliminates the need for insurance claim processing Cash-only services may limit access to care for patients without adequate financial resources It may attract patients who value privacy and discretion in their healthcare Cash-only practices must still adhere to federal and state regulations, including privacy and anti-discrimination laws Some physicians have expressed concerns about the sustainability of cash-only practices

Adapting Compliance Strategies for U.S Expansion Expansion strategy should consider the differing healthcare landscapes across U.S states A comprehensive risk management plan should be developed to mitigate potential issues An effective communication strategy is essential to inform stakeholders of the expansion Regulatory compliance should be a key focus during the expansion process Consider potential changes to the business model to adapt to U.S healthcare practices Post-expansion, it is important to monitor and adjust strategies based on evolving regulations and market conditions
