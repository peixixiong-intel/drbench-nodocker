Overview of Regulatory Compliance in Virtual Healthcare Regulatory compliance in virtual healthcare primarily involves adhering to privacy laws and standards In Canada, this is governed by the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) Other key regulations include the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) for services provided to US patients Telemedicine platforms must also comply with various technical standards to ensure secure data transmission Regulatory compliance is a key factor in maintaining patient trust and avoiding legal issues Non-compliance can result in substantial fines and potential loss of license to operate Continuous monitoring and reporting are crucial components of a robust compliance strategy
