AI-Integrated ITSM for Enhanced Remote Healthcare

Slide 2:
Introduction As MediConn Solutions continues to expand its digital services, incorporating AI capabilities into our IT Service Management can help elevate our remote care experience. This presentation will explore how AI can enhance our support for mobile health apps and uphold our commitment to regulatory compliance.

Slide 3:
Current ITSM Challenges in Remote Care In Q1 of 2025, we experienced significant network disruptions, leading to a total of 350 minutes of service downtime that impacted our remote care delivery. The service downtime compromised our ability to provide a seamless experience for patients and healthcare professionals. With the current ITSM approach, we are challenged in mitigating such service disruptions, thereby affecting our overall service quality. Our existing IT staff were stretched thin due to the high volume of tickets, impacting the resolution time for recurring issues. Implementing AI-integrated ITSM may alleviate these challenges by automating the resolution of common issues, freeing up IT staff for more complex problems. Automated evidence collection and audit preparation facilitated by AI-integrated ITSM will also enable us to maintain regulatory compliance more efficiently. A transition to an AI-integrated ITSM model has the potential to reduce service downtime, ensuring a more reliable remote care experience.

AI-Driven Solutions for IT Service Management AI can help automate repetitive tasks and free up ITSM teams to focus on complex issues AI can help in predictive maintenance by identifying system vulnerabilities beforehand AI can facilitate real-time monitoring of systems to ensure they are functioning optimally AI can help in data analysis to derive insights and improve service delivery AI can assist in managing and prioritizing service requests effectively AI can enhance cybersecurity measures by detecting and mitigating threats in real time
