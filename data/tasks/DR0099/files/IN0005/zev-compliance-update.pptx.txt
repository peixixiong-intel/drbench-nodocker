Overview of Canada’s Revised ZEV Mandate In order to align with Canada's updated ZEV mandate, Elexion Automotive needs to revamp its compliance documentation framework. A critical aspect of this transition involves ensuring our compliance staff are adequately trained on the revised verification protocols. The training scope is expected to encompass approximately 55 of our compliance personnel. These personnel will collectively require around 1,200 hours of training to fully comprehend the new protocols. By Q2 2026, we need to have completed this training to avoid creating audit gaps. The training timeline, in turn, will impact our budget and scheduling decisions for the fiscal year 2026. Therefore, it is crucial that we structure our compliance documentation framework in a manner that allows sufficient time for staff training.
