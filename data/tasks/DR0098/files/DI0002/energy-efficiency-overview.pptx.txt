Energy Efficiency in Lee's Market Operations

Slide 2:
Introduction This presentation will provide an overview of our recent energy efficiency initiatives, with a focus on the upgrade of refrigeration systems in 20 of our stores in Q1 2025. The aim is to understand the impact of these upgrades on our energy consumption.

Slide 3:
Overview of Energy Efficiency Initiatives In Q4 2024, Lee's Market committed to a comprehensive energy optimization program Across the industry, energy costs represent, on average, 2-3% of retailers’ total operating costs Lee's Market has prioritized upgrading lighting systems in its stores The company has also invested in energy-efficient heating and cooling systems A dedicated sustainability team was established in early 2025 to oversee these initiatives The team is also responsible for conducting regular energy audits and training staff on energy conservation techniques

Slide 4:
Impact of Refrigeration System Upgrades Upgrading refrigeration systems can lead to energy savings of 20-50% Lee's Market implemented a training program for staff to properly manage the new systems The company also conducted a comprehensive evaluation of its refrigeration equipment in Q4 2024 The new refrigeration systems are expected to have a lifespan of 15-20 years The company is also analyzing the possibility of investing in renewable energy sources to power these systems

Slide 5:
Next Steps in Energy Optimization Lee's Market plans to implement smart meters in all stores by Q3 2025 The company is also exploring partnerships with local renewable energy providers A plan is in place to upgrade the HVAC systems in the next fiscal year Lee's Market aims to achieve a 40% reduction in overall energy consumption by 2027 The company also plans to extend its energy efficiency training program to all employees

Slide 6:
Conclusion The upgrade of our refrigeration systems has resulted in significant reductions in energy consumption. We will continue to pursue energy-efficient technologies for further improvements in our operations.
