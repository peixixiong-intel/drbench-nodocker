Overview of Revenue Cycle Management Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) is essential for maintaining financial stability in healthcare organizations RCM comprises various stages including patient pre-registration, claim submission, and final payment Advanced RCM systems can help in identifying unpaid claims and minimizing claim denials RCM implementation requires staff training and technology investment Successful RCM can lead to increased patient satisfaction and better resource allocation Adoption of RCM has been a rising trend in the healthcare industry since 2010 By 2024, the global RCM market is projected to reach $90 billion
