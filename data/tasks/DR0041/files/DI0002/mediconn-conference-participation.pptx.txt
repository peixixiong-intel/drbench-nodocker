Introduction This presentation aims to review MediConn's participation in important healthcare conferences. Such participation is strategic as it gives us exposure and access to trending market developments.

Slide 3:
Overview of Key Healthcare Conferences The Healthcare Information and Management Systems Society (HIMSS) Global Conference is a key event for health information and technology professionals. The American Telemedicine Association (ATA) organizes an annual conference focusing on telehealth innovations. The Connected Health Conference focuses on the most pressing actionable technology and health trends. The mHealth Summit, held annually, provides a platform for healthcare professionals to discuss mobile, telehealth, and connected health initiatives. The World Health Care Congress (WHCC) hosts an annual event bringing together global healthcare stakeholders to share best practices. The e-Health Annual Conference &amp; Tradeshow is Canada's largest digital health event.

Slide 4:
MediConn's Conference Participation in 2025 MediConn Solutions participated in the ATA annual conference in May 2025. The company presented a case study at the Connected Health Conference in June 2025. MediConn Solutions was represented at the e-Health Annual Conference &amp; Tradeshow. The company did not participate in the HIMSS Global Conference or the mHealth Summit in 2025. MediConn Solutions participated in several regional healthcare conferences across Canada in 2025.

Impact of Conference Participation on Marketing Strategy Conference participation has facilitated networking with potential clients and partners. It has provided insights into the latest healthcare trends and technologies. The company has leveraged conference exposure to enhance its brand visibility. Conference participation has helped MediConn to benchmark its services against industry standards. The company has gained insights into market needs which have informed product development. Business leads generated from conference participation have been integrated into the sales pipeline.
