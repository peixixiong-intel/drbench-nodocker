Introduction This presentation analyses our recent workforce expansion, focusing on the impact of our employee referral program. It provides insights on the effectiveness of our referral program in our hiring process.

Q2 Workforce Expansion Overview Total hiring in Q2 increased by 12% compared to Q1 Majority of new hires joined the technical and customer support departments A comprehensive onboarding process was initiated for all new hires The average experience of new hires in Q2 was 5.3 years The company introduced a new screening process in Q2 to improve the quality of hires The attrition rate observed in Q2 was 3%, lower than industry average

Slide 4:
Contribution of Employee Referral Program The employee referral program was introduced in 2018 The program offers a bonus to employees who refer candidates that are successfully hired The program has been successful in attracting high-quality candidates A survey revealed that employees find the referral program easy to use The program has seen steady participation since its inception In 2020, the program has undergone a few enhancements to make it more attractive

Slide 5:
Improving the Effectiveness of Referral Program An online portal was introduced in Q1 to simplify the referral process Employee training sessions were conducted in Q2 to promote the program A feedback mechanism is in place to continually improve the program There are plans to increase the referral bonus in Q3 to motivate more employees The company is considering partnering with external agencies to boost the program The company is exploring the idea of 'referral drives' to amplify participation

Slide 6:
Conclusion Our employee referral program has significantly contributed to our recent hiring, but there is scope for improvement. We must leverage this potential to enhance our hiring process and achieve cost-effective recruitment.
