Evolving Cyber Threat Landscape and Anomaly Detection An issue previously encountered during AI integration was an increase in false positives, leading to the unnecessary isolation of crucial medical systems. This necessitates the development of a robust filtering system to distinguish genuine threats from benign anomalies. Over time, model bias or drift was identified, leading to unnoticed real threats - a potential risk that needs to be addressed during the AI integration phase. The failure to integrate data with legacy hospital systems was another significant challenge in monitoring coverage. To prevent this, ensuring seamless data integration between the new AI system and existing infrastructure will be essential. Given the importance of uninterrupted healthcare service, it is critical to address these issues proactively to prevent operational disruptions. It is important to revisit and refine our approach periodically to adapt to the evolving cyber threat landscape.
