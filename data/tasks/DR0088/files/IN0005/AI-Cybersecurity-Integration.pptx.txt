Understanding Canada's 2025 CSCP Guidelines The integration of AI anomaly detection tools aligns with Canadian CSCP guidelines for bolstered cyber resilience by 2025. To ensure the seamless integration of new AI tools, MediConn is devising a comprehensive staging environment. This staging environment mimics our live production system, offering a safe testing ground for the new AI tools. Hosting this staging environment on a separate cloud infrastructure, we aim to prevent any potential disruptions to patient services. The isolation of the staging environment allows for rigorous security testing without the risk of service downtime. This strategy ensures that the integration process of the new AI system doesn't interfere with MediConn's current workflow. By implementing this approach, MediConn aligns with Canada’s strategic initiatives to strengthen its cyber resilience.
