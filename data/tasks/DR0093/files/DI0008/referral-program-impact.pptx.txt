2024 Referral Program Overview The referral program was launched in Q2 2024 to attract new patients The program was promoted primarily through social media and email marketing Incentives offered included discounts on future consultations The program was managed by the customer relations department The referral process was automated through the MediConn app The referral program was well received with high user engagement The program was aligned with industry standards and regulations

New Patient Registrations via Referral Referrals became a major source of new patient registrations in 2024 A significant proportion of new patients were introduced through existing patients The demographics of referred patients were diverse, with a majority in the 30-45 age bracket Feedback from referred patients indicated high satisfaction with the registration process Referred patients showed high engagement rates with our digital health services The customer relations team facilitated the integration of new referrals into our systems

Referral Program Enhancements for Future Growth Future enhancements to the program include expanding to more marketing channels The feedback loop will be improved for better understanding of patient needs The referral reward system will be optimized based on patient feedback We are considering partnerships with pharmacies for better reward offerings The app interface will be updated for easier referral process The registration process for referred patients will be further simplified The success metrics for the program will be reevaluated and updated
