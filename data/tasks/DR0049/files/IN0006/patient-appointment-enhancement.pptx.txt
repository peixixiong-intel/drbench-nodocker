Overview of Current Appointment Booking System In Q4 2024, our customer satisfaction scores for support interactions averaged 3.2 out of 5, reflecting areas for improvement. A reported 58% of negative reviews pointed to delays in reaching a representative as a primary concern. This delay in customer support interaction could be contributing to current barriers in booking appointments. Current bottlenecks in customer support may indicate an over-reliance on our call centers. These findings suggest that our customer support process may need streamlining to improve accessibility. Addressing these issues may better position us to meet patient needs and improve our overall patient experience. Strategically, these insights could guide efforts to enhance our digital platform and customer support process.
