Introduction Our commitment to providing accessible healthcare has been challenged by recent feedback on our booking processes. This presentation will explore the potential issues and identify improvement strategies.

Slide 3:
Analyzing MediConn's Current Booking System Our Q4 2024 data shows that customers face an average delay of 12 minutes to reach a customer service representative during peak hours. In some instances, this wait time escalates to as high as 25 minutes, potentially causing frustration and impacting our customer satisfaction ratings. These delays are most pronounced during peak hours, which run from 9 AM to 12 PM EST. High wait times might be indicative of convoluted customer service processes that need streamlining or the lack of efficient patient-facing care management tools. The report from PatientEngagementHIT highlights that 60% of patients struggle with poor online booking tools and complex call center procedures. We need to explore whether the root cause lies in our booking platform's design or in the customer support processes. The goal should be to reduce wait times and improve the patient experience by simplifying processes or enhancing our digital tools.

Slide 4:
Evaluating Customer Support Interaction Processes MediConn's customer support department is made up of 50 representatives The department is divided into two shifts to provide 24/7 support The customer support team utilizes CRM software, CareSupport, to manage calls Representatives undergo quarterly training for soft skills and technical knowledge The average call duration is approximately 4 minutes The customer support department resolved over 10,000 tickets in 2021 The department has a first-call resolution rate of 78%

Slide 5:
Identifying Root Causes of Patient Booking Struggles In 2021, 3% of all calls were related to booking struggles The majority of booking struggles were reported by patients over the age of 60 Most commonly, booking struggles were due to internet connectivity issues Phone booking requests are handled by a dedicated team of 10 representatives The company conducts bi-annual surveys to understand patient experiences MediConn offers a user guide and tutorial videos to assist with online booking Less than 1% of patients reported struggles with the corporate booking feature
