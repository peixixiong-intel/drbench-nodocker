Optimizing Patient Appointment Experience

Slide 2:
Introduction In light of recent feedback on our appointment booking process, we need to critically examine and identify the areas that require improvement. This presentation aims to evaluate whether our booking tool design or customer support processes are hindering patient experience.

Slide 3:
Analyzing Patient Feedback on Booking Tools In Q4 2024, 11% of all booking attempts, equating to 5,200 users, discontinued their booking attempt at the coverage verification phase. These users ceased their booking process specifically during provincial health card and supplemental insurance validation. Interestingly, these users made no attempts to contact our support team, implying a potential confusion or frustration with the process. This abandonment could indicate a lack of clarity or user-friendliness in our coverage verification step. This data prompts us to consider the need for a more intuitive interface or additional help resources at this critical juncture in the booking process. We need to investigate if this pattern is consistent across different periods or if it is a unique occurrence in Q4 2024. Understanding the root cause of this issue is vital to improving our platform's usability and overall patient experience.
