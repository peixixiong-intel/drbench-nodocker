Introduction As we prepare for the launch of our 2025 model, it's crucial to understand the findings from our recent durability tests. This presentation focuses on the suspension component failures detected during these tests.

Overview of 2025 Model Durability Test The durability test was conducted over a period of 120 days A total of 15 prototypes of the 2025 model were put through the test The test simulated a variety of driving conditions, from urban traffic to rough terrains The prototypes were driven for an average of 18 hours per day during the test Sophisticated tracking technology was used to monitor all aspects of the vehicles' performance The test was carried out in a controlled environment to ensure consistent results An independent third-party agency was used to verify the integrity of the testing process

Slide 4:
Suspension Component Failures Analysis The suspension components were sourced from a reputable supplier All components were thoroughly inspected prior to installation The components were designed for maximum durability and performance The testing process included regular checks on the suspension system Despite rigorous quality control measures, failures can occur in any complex mechanical system A detailed failure analysis will be carried out to determine the exact cause of the failures Recommendations will be made to prevent similar failures in future models

Slide 5:
Impact on Production Timeline and Costs Any component failure can potentially delay the production timeline A delay in production can result in increased costs The company has contingency plans in place to mitigate such risks The cost of replacing faulty components is factored into the overall production budget The company is committed to delivering high-quality products, even if it means incurring additional costs The impact on the production timeline and costs will be thoroughly assessed Measures will be taken to minimize any negative impact on the production timeline and costs

Conclusion Our analysis reveals certain challenges in the suspension components of our 2025 model. Moving forward, we need to prioritize these concerns during the redesign phase to ensure a successful and punctual launch.
