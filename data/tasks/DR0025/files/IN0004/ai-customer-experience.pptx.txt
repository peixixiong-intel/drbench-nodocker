Introduction As we aim to enhance our shoppers' experiences, we are exploring the potential of AI tools such as chatbots and conversational AI. This presentation will outline how these technologies can personalize the customer journey through 2030 and beyond.

Slide 3:
Current Customer Service Landscape at Lee's Market In the month of June 2024, we saw a significant shift in our customer service interactions, with about 65% of our recurring shopper base utilizing digital channels. The majority of these digital interactions occurred through our messaging apps and social media platforms, surpassing traditional methods of communication such as phone and email. With the rising preference for digital, we must consider the global chatbot market, which is predicted to expand from $2.9 billion in 2021 to $10.5 billion by 2026, at a 23.5% CAGR. The Grocery Doppio report indicates that over 62% of grocery shoppers appreciate fast, real-time support provided by AI chats or virtual assistants, aligning with our own observation. Our current digital customer service strategy aligns with market trends, but we need to further enhance our AI capabilities to meet the increasing demand. The integration of AI tools, including chatbots and conversational AI, will cater to our customers’ evolving preferences and can significantly elevate the shopping experience for our customers.
