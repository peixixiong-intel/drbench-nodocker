Introduction As part of our ongoing effort to improve Lee's Market's customer experience, this presentation will explore the potential of AI tools for personalizing the shopping journey. We will delve into how chatbots and conversational AI can optimize our online and in-store service offerings.

Slide 3:
Current State of Customer Service at Lee's Market Our internal analytics showcase a notable online consumer usage of our product search tool, with approximately 55% of our e-shoppers leveraging it as of October 1, 2024. However, only a fraction of these users, less than 20%, utilize the recipes or meal suggestions feature when purchasing groceries online. This data suggests there is room to enhance customer engagement with our online features, particularly the recipe suggestions. The underutilization of the recipe feature could be an opportunity to incorporate AI chatbots and conversational AI for personalized recipe suggestions. With the global chatbot market predicted to rise significantly by 2026, there's potential for Lee's Market to leverage this technology to improve customer service. Given that over 60% of grocery shoppers prefer automated tools for real-time support, implementing AI tools can meet customer expectations and improve their shopping experience. Our strategy going forward should focus on personalizing shoppers' experiences through advanced AI tools to increase the usage of online features.
