Slide 3:
Current Customer Service Landscape As of Q4 2025, customer service agents at Lee's Market spent an average of 1.8 minutes per call collecting redundant client information. This time consumption represents 17% of the average call duration for escalated tickets. The repetition of customer information results in a significant waste of resources, negatively impacting customer experience. The introduction of a unified Customer Data Platform (CDP) by Q2 2026 would help streamline this process. The CDP implementation is projected to save approximately 1.1 minutes per call, enhancing efficiency. This reduction in call time is expected to translate into an estimated annual labor cost saving of $75,000 in the contact center. The savings and efficiency gains will allow agents to focus more on personalized customer interactions, improving overall customer experience.
