Introduction The purpose of this presentation is to analyze the recent trends in employee unionization at Lee's Market. Our focus will be on evaluating the percentage of employees who were part of a union as of Q2 2026.

Slide 3:
Evolution of Employee Unionization Unionization efforts began in the company in the early 2000s By 2010, 30% of employees were part of a union In 2015, the company reached a peak of 45% unionization Unionization decreased to 35% in 2020 following changes in company policy As of 2025, management implemented new strategies to maintain a healthy relationship with unions Unions have played a significant role in shaping employee benefits and wage structures

Slide 4:
Unionized Employee Percentage - Q2 2026 The total number of employees in Q2 2026 was 5,000 A significant percentage of employees were part of the retail department The customer service department had the lowest number of unionized employees Unionized employees were primarily full-time rather than part-time The average age of unionized employees was 35 years The gender distribution among unionized employees was nearly equal

Slide 5:
Impact of Unionization on Employee Satisfaction Employee satisfaction surveys indicate a positive correlation with unionization Unionized employees reported higher job security Non-unionized employees had higher flexibility in job roles Overall job satisfaction was not significantly different between unionized and non-unionized employees Unionized employees reported increased satisfaction with dispute resolution processes Both groups indicated room for improvement in communication with management
