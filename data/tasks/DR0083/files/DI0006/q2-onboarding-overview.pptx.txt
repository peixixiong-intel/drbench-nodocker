New Hires Breakdown in Q2 A total of 250 new hires joined Elexion Automotive in Q2 The Engineering department saw the highest influx of new hires, with 80 new employees The Sales and Marketing departments recruited 70 and 40 new members respectively R&amp;D department had 30 new hires, while the remaining were scattered among other departments The average age of new hires was 28 years, with a 60-40 male to female ratio Majority of new hires were sourced from job portals and referrals

Q2 HR Onboarding Sessions Analysis The HR department introduced a new onboarding program in Q2 The new onboarding program focused on integrating company culture and values It involved department heads as speakers to give an overview of their respective departments The feedback received for the new onboarding program was predominantly positive The program included a tour of the facility and an introduction to the company's mission and vision A mentorship initiative was also introduced as part of the onboarding program

Impact of Onboarding on Employee Efficiency Employee efficiency showed an upward trend post onboarding The new onboarding program was found to decrease the time taken by new hires to adapt to their roles Surveys suggested that new hires felt more welcomed and prepared after the onboarding process A 15% improvement in productivity was observed in new hires after attending the onboarding sessions The well-structured onboarding program also helped to reduce the attrition rate among new hires The feedback from department heads indicated that the onboarding program helped new hires integrate faster
