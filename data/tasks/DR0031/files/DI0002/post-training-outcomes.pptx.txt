Introduction This presentation provides an overview of changes recorded in warehouse operations at Lee's Market after the 2024 employee training. It aims to assess the efficacy of our training programs in improving operational efficiency.

Slide 3:
2024 Employee Training Overview The training was conducted in November 2024 in all regional centers The focus was on customer service, product knowledge, and store operations The training was delivered in a blended format - online and in-person The training was developed in-house by Lee's Market's HR and Training departments Employees from all departments, including sales, cashiering, and merchandise stocking, participated in the training The training was part of Lee's Market's commitment to continuous employee development Feedback from the employees indicated a high level of satisfaction with the training content and delivery

Post-Training Operational Improvements Customer service ratings have improved by 4% in the first quarter of 2025 There has been a 6% increase in online sales since the beginning of 2025 Store cleanliness ratings have improved by 5% since the training Average checkout times have reduced by 2 minutes since December 2024 There has been a 3% reduction in product returns in the first quarter of 2025 Employee turnover has reduced by 10% in the first quarter of 2025

Conclusion Our recent training session has made considerable improvements in warehouse operations, reflecting the value of continuous employee development. Moving forward, we must focus on areas that still need improvement and continue to invest in our training programs.
