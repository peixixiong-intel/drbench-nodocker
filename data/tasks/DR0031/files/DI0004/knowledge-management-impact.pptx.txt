Introduction This presentation evaluates the impact of the knowledge management team's efforts in refining the process repository access in February 2025. We specifically focus on its influence on the content creation process within the marketing department.

Exploring the Process Repository Enhancements The repository underwent an interface overhaul for increased user-friendliness Enhanced search capabilities were implemented to quickly locate resources The new information architecture was based on the ISO 15489 Records Management standard A digital rights management system was integrated to protect intellectual property An automated system for document version control was introduced A dedicated team was assigned to ensure the repository's regular maintenance and updates

Slide 4:
Content Creation: Pre and Post Implementation Prior to the update, the average time spent on locating resources was 45 minutes The number of unique daily users of the repository increased by 30% post-update The amount of redundant content creation has been reduced by an estimated 70% Repository downtime decreased from 2 hours monthly to 10 minutes The average time for creating new content has decreased by 2 hours post enhancements

Marketing Staff Feedback on Process Changes The new repository received a user satisfaction rate of 88% in the first quarter Marketing employees report a 30% increase in collaborative projects The marketing team reported a 15% increase in productivity post implementation Staff training on the new repository took an average of 1.5 hours per employee The most appreciated feature among marketing staff was the improved search function
