Analyzing Canada's Current Retail Landscape We observed a retail sales decline by 0.8% in Canada during July 2025, impacting Lee's Market's performance. To counter this, we focused on enhancing on-floor customer engagement, particularly through personalized product recommendations. An encouraging finding from this strategy was that in August 2025, 35% of customers who were provided with personalized recommendations in the previous month returned to make additional purchases. This indicates the effectiveness of personalized engagement, suggesting we should continue to focus on offering tailored advice to customers. Further analysis will be conducted to understand the detailed impact of the strategy on sales recovery and customer retention. A measurable rebound in the remaining months of 2025 will be sought by refining and expanding the personalized recommendation initiative. Additional training for sales associates could be implemented to enhance their ability to provide effective, personalized product suggestions.
