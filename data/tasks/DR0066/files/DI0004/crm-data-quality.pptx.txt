Introduction In the pursuit of more effective marketing and sales strategies, we have conducted an analysis of our CRM data quality. This presentation will shed light on the current state of our data and propose strategies for enhancement.

Slide 3:
Current State of CRM Data Our CRM system was last updated in March 2024 with a new interface and improved user experience The CRM system is currently used by all departments across Elexion Automotive We have seen a 12% increase in CRM usage since the last quarter of 2024 Training modules for CRM usage have been developed and are mandatory for all new hires The CRM system is synced with our social media platforms for better customer interaction A recent survey revealed 80% of our employees find the CRM system easy to use and intuitive The CRM system has a dedicated IT support team to address technical issues in real time

Slide 4:
Impact of Incomplete CRM Data Incomplete CRM data can lead to inaccurate customer insights, impacting marketing strategies It can result in inefficient communication with customers, affecting customer satisfaction levels Incomplete data might make it difficult to track customer behavior and preferences It could lead to missed sales opportunities due to lack of personalized communication Incomplete CRM data can affect the effectiveness of email marketing campaigns Poor data quality can lead to wrong decisions due to inaccurate reports

Slide 5:
Strategies for Enhancing CRM Data Regular data audits can help identify missing or inaccurate data Implementing a data validation process during data entry can reduce errors Training employees on the importance of data completeness can improve data quality Integrating the CRM system with other business systems can ensure data consistency Using data cleaning tools can help remove duplicates and correct inaccurate data Implementing a data governance strategy can ensure data management best practices are followed

Slide 6:
Conclusion Our CRM data currently contains a significant percentage of incomplete entries, which affects our marketing and sales efforts. To improve data quality, we need to implement a comprehensive data management strategy and regularly monitor data integrity.
