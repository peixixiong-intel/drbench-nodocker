Understanding Current CRM Data Utilization Our CRM system shows an overall 4.8% conversion rate for qualified digital leads across all channels as of October 2023. With the emerging shift towards online car purchasing, our digital sales channels are becoming increasingly important for Elexion's business model. The 4.8% conversion rate provides opportunities to improve our online sales process and increase customer engagement. Given the industry trend towards direct-to-customer sales, optimizing our online sales funnel could significantly boost the lead-to-sale conversion rate. By examining the journey of our converted leads, we can identify successful digital touchpoints and replicate this success across other channels. It's critical to analyze the behavior of the 95.2% of digital leads that do not convert to increase our conversion rate moving forward. Leveraging data from our CRM system, we can build a predictive model to identify high potential leads and streamline our sales process.
