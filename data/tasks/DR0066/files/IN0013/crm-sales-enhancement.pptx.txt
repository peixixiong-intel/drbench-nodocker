Slide 3:
Understanding our Customer Profile Our CRM data suggests a higher conversion rate for personalized emails containing vehicle configuration recommendations. Over Q3 2024, the conversion rate for such personalized content stood at 9.7%. In the same period, generic content emails witnessed a significantly lower conversion at 5.3%. This resulted in a stark difference in unit sales - 234 units sold via personalized emails compared to 167 through generic content. The highest impact was observed when such personalized content reached the potential customers within 10 days of their initial inquiry. This indicates a clear preference among our customers for personalized and timely communication. Leveraging this insight could potentially enhance our online sales strategy and the overall customer experience.
