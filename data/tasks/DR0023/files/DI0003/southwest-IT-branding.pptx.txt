Introduction This presentation provides an overview of IT-led brand reinforcement initiatives within our Southwest stores, focusing specifically on the installation of brand-aligned keyboard LED lights during September 2025.

Slide 3:
Overview of IT-Driven Branding Initiatives IT-Driven Branding Initiatives began in 2022, focusing on in-store digital displays Deployment of RFID technology to track in-store customer behavior started in 2023 The integration of QR codes into product labels for additional product information was initiated in 2024 AI-driven chatbot was introduced in 2025 to enhance customer service The use of Augmented Reality for in-store navigation is under consideration for 2026 IT and Marketing departments collaborate closely for these initiatives, meeting bi-weekly to discuss progress and feedback

Slide 4:
LED Installation Requests - September 2025 LED installations are handled by a dedicated IT sub-team The LED installation process includes a site survey, planning, and final installation LED installations typically take two weeks from request to completion The LED installations are part of a wider store modernization initiative LED installations have been completed in over 50 stores nationwide as of September 2025 Each LED installation is followed by a post-installation review to ensure quality and effectiveness

Slide 5:
Impact and Evaluation of Brand-Aligned LEDs Brand-aligned LEDs have been positively received by customers in pilot stores Store associates report increased customer engagement with brand-aligned LEDs Brand-aligned LEDs have contributed to an overall modern aesthetic in the stores Initial data show an increase in in-store traffic after LED installations A more formal evaluation of the impact of brand-aligned LEDs is planned for Q4 2025 The cost-benefit analysis of the LED installations will be part of the annual IT review in 2026

Slide 6:
Conclusion The led lighting initiative has been successful in reinforcing our brand identity in the Southwest region. Moving forward, we should explore other avenues for IT-driven brand enhancements.
