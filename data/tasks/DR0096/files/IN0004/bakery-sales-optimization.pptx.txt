Introduction In light of the recent rise in food service sales, this presentation aims to explore strategies to optimize our in-store layouts and product displays, particularly focusing on our bakery department. The ultimate goal is to leverage these changes to achieve a 15% increase in bakery sales by the end of Q1 2026.

Slide 3:
Understanding Current Retail Trends in Food Service The initial point of customer engagement, our entrance-facing bread display, has demonstrated strong sales performance with an impressive $1,250 per square foot from June to September 2025. Our cake and pastry display holds its own with $1,100 in sales per square foot, indicating a continued consumer interest in these products. The checkout-side impulse rack, although not as high performing as the initial bread display, still generated substantial sales of $900 per square foot. The beverage adjacency shelf, while currently underperforming at $650 per square foot, presents an opportunity for strategic merchandising enhancements to boost sales. Our custom cake counter, despite being a less-trafficked zone, has shown remarkable productivity with the highest sales per square foot of $1,500. These data points provide crucial insights into our spatial efficiency and serve as a baseline for evaluating the effectiveness of our in-store layouts and product displays. Using this data, it's possible to optimize our in-store layouts and increase bakery sales by 15% by the end of Q1 2026.
