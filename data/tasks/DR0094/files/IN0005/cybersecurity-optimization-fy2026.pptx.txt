Evolving Cyber Threats in Digital Healthcare In our FY2025 security audit, there was identification of 7 major system threats that pose a critical risk to our data security. Additionally, 18 vulnerabilities of a medium-level nature were discovered during the audit, pointing towards areas requiring immediate attention. The audit findings indicate an immediate need to prioritize patching and threat mitigation efforts in FY2026 to address these vulnerabilities. The critical and medium-level vulnerabilities combined present a potential threat to our digital engagement and operational efficiency. It is essential to invest in advanced threat detection and prevention technologies to mitigate these vulnerabilities. Strengthening our internal security controls can better safeguard our systems and patient data. Ongoing staff training on cybersecurity best practices will also play an essential role in preventing future vulnerabilities.
