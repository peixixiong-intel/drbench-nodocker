Understanding Centennial Shoppers' Expectations Our social media channels experienced an influx of around 100 new followers each month, during the first quarter of 2025. Crucially, these online interactions were not merely passive, with roughly a quarter of these new followers being converted into active purchasers. Our use of conversational AI chatbots has proven instrumental in this conversion process, providing personalized recommendations to each new follower. The successful engagement with these new customers is an indication of the effectiveness of the implemented AI solutions. The chatbots have not only enhanced our customer engagement but have also improved the efficiency of our customer service operations. Moving forward, we need to continuously optimize our AI tools to maintain and increase these conversion rates. It is crucial to leverage these tools further for better customer engagement, thus driving loyalty among our Centennial shoppers.

Conversational AI in Customer Service: Opportunities and Challenges AI can streamline customer service processes and improve efficiency. AI can be programmed to handle multiple languages and dialects. AI implementation requires significant investment in technology and training. There are concerns about AI's ability to understand and respond to complex inquiries. It can be challenging to ensure the AI is accurately representing the brand and its values.

Slide 5:
Securing Customer Interactions in the AI Era Ensuring data privacy is a top priority in customer interactions. Strict compliance with data protection regulations is necessary. Regular audits of data handling practices can help maintain security. Employee training on cybersecurity best practices is crucial. Implementing advanced security measures like encryption and two-factor authentication can enhance security.

Slide 6:
Conclusion By leveraging conversational AI, we can significantly improve customer engagement and loyalty among our key demographic, the Centennial shoppers. However, we must ensure the secure handling of customer interactions to maintain trust and compliance.
