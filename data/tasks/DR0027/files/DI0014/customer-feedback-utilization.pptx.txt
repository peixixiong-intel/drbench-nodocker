Introduction This presentation explores our Q2 2024 strategy on leveraging customer feedback to enhance our online customer service. We will also review its impact on our response time and overall customer satisfaction.

Slide 3:
Overview of Q2 2024 Customer Feedback In Q2 2024, Lee’s Market received over 2,000 customer feedback forms per week 65% of the feedback was related to in-store shopping experiences The feedback ranged across various store departments, including produce, bakery, and seafood 30% of responses pointed towards product quality and pricing Feedback forms were presented in multiple languages, including English, Mandarin, Korean and Vietnamese Over 70% of the customers used the digital feedback form available on the website

Slide 4:
Implementation of Feedback in Customer Service The customer service team held weekly meetings to review and analyze feedback Feedback was categorized into different themes for clearer understanding Efforts were made to improve in-store experiences based on feedback The customer service team underwent training to handle feedback more effectively Customer service prioritized feedback that had the highest frequency

Slide 5:
Impact on Online Customer Inquiry Response Time The average response time for online inquiries was 24 hours in Q2 2024 There was a 10% increase in online customer inquiries in Q2 2024 compared to Q1 2024 Lee’s Market hired additional customer service staff to handle the increased volume of inquiries The company integrated a new AI chatbot to assist with handling customer inquiries The new chatbot was responsible for handling 15% of all online inquiries

Slide 6:
Conclusion Through strategic use of customer feedback, we managed to significantly reduce our online inquiry response time. Moving forward, we must continue to leverage this feedback to improve our services and maintain customer satisfaction.
