Revamping Lee's Market Loyalty Program

Slide 2:
Introduction As we aim to redesign our loyalty program, it's crucial to balance the preferences of both our older and younger customer demographics. This presentation explores key considerations for this process.

Slide 3:
Understanding Gen Z/Millennial Digital Preferences Current customer demographics reveal Gen Z and Millennials constitute 62% of Lee's Market's active customer base as of Q4 2025. This trend signifies a shift towards a younger, digitally inclined customer base, necessitating a digital-first loyalty scheme. Gen X and Boomer shoppers, though lower in proportions (38%), are a revenue-stable segment, requiring consideration in the loyalty program design. A balanced approach is essential to cater to both the tech-savvy Gen Z/Millennial customer base and traditional Gen X/Boomer customers. The loyalty program redesign should incorporate digital strategies that resonate with younger customers, including mobile apps and social media initiatives. Simultaneously, traditional engagement methods should be maintained to satisfy Gen X/Boomer customers' preferences. Continuous monitoring and evaluation of customer preferences across all generations will ensure our loyalty program remains flexible and adaptable.

Slide 4:
Meeting Gen X/Boomer Traditional Service Expectations Gen X/Boomers appreciate physical store experiences and human customer service They value quality over quantity and are less likely to be influenced by discounts This demographic tends to be brand loyal and appreciates personalized service Direct mail and email are preferred modes of communication Gen X/Boomers still appreciate traditional payment methods like cash and credit cards They value the convenience of home delivery and in-store pick-up options

Slide 5:
Balancing Traditional and Digital in Loyalty Program Offering both digital and physical loyalty cards can satisfy diverse customer preferences Incorporating personalized offers in both online and offline channels can enhance customer experience Ensuring seamless integration between online and in-store shopping can improve customer convenience Providing options for digital and traditional payments can cater to different demographic preferences Maintaining a balance between instant gratification and long-term rewards can keep customers engaged

Slide 6:
Conclusion By understanding and integrating the preferences of both our younger and older customer base, we can create a loyalty program that appeals to all. The next step involves refining our program based on these insights and piloting it in select stores.
