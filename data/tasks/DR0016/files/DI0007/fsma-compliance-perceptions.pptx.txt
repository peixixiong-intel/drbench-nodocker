Understanding FSMA 204: An Overview FSMA 204 is a federal law enacted in 2011 to ensure food safety It shifts the focus of federal regulators from responding to contamination to preventing it This legislation is applicable to all companies that manufacture, process, pack, or hold food It mandates companies to have a written preventive controls plan This plan should outline how the company will prevent food safety hazards It should also include a plan for correcting any problems that could arise The law also includes mandatory inspections by the FDA
