Overview of Digital Asset Lifecycle Management Digital Asset Lifecycle Management (DALM) is a critical component of Elexion's tech-driven approach It involves the tracking, management, and eventual decommissioning of digital assets DALM at Elexion includes asset creation, update, active usage, decommissioning, and archival The DALM team utilizes advanced software tools to manage assets efficiently A cross-functional team of developers, designers, and project managers oversees DALM Timely decommissioning of unused assets reduces system clutter and optimizes server resources Consistent asset management strategies ensure regulatory compliance across multiple jurisdictions

Slide 4:
Decommissioning Analysis: March - December 2024 Decommissioning is a crucial phase of DALM, ensuring efficient resource utilization The decommissioning process is overseen by a dedicated team led by Olivia Martinez Regular audits are conducted to identify inactive or redundant assets for decommissioning Decommissioning decisions are based on usage analytics, stakeholder input, and strategic considerations Elexion uses a phased decommissioning process to minimize disruption Post-decommissioning, assets are archived securely for potential future reference The decommissioning team maintains detailed records in compliance with data management regulations

Slide 5:
Impact of Asset Decommissioning on Operational Efficiency Efficient decommissioning can lead to significant improvements in operational efficiency It helps to streamline digital workflows, reducing system load and improving performance By decommissioning unused assets, Elexion can reallocate resources to more critical areas Decommissioning also plays a role in maintaining system security, as outdated assets can pose risks It contributes to cost savings by reducing the need for additional server space Periodic decommissioning of assets is part of Elexion's strategy for sustainable operations The decommissioning process also includes stakeholder communication to ensure smooth transitions
