Maintenance Work Order Overview Elexion Automotive has a robust process for managing maintenance work orders The system is designed to streamline requests, prioritization, and assignment of tasks Our maintenance department is comprised of 75 skilled professionals We've seen a 10% increase in the overall volume of work orders over the last two years The average time to complete a work order is 3-5 days On average, each technician completes 15-20 work orders per month

Breakdown of Closed Work Orders Our maintenance work orders cover a wide range of tasks, from equipment repairs to routine inspections Approximately 60% of work orders are for preventative maintenance The remaining 40% are typically for repairs or upgrades The most common issues requiring maintenance are related to production machinery We've invested heavily in training programs to reduce the frequency of these issues Our goal is to continue reducing the number of repair-related work orders

Impact on Plant Productivity Effective maintenance management is crucial to maintaining high levels of plant productivity We've seen a direct correlation between reduced maintenance times and increased productivity Our predictive maintenance strategies have helped to reduce downtime by 15% in the last year We've also noticed a decrease in equipment failures and production stoppages Our goal is to continue streamlining our maintenance processes to further increase productivity
