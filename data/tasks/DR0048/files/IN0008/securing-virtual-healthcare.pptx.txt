The successful identification and tracking of these devices underlines the robustness of our current ITSM security protocols. Through these protocols, we can maintain a proactive approach in identifying potential IT issues and securing sensitive medical data. The increased number of devices in our ITSM inventory system reflects our commitment to the expansion and improvement of our virtual healthcare services. Our future focus will be on continuing to enhance ITSM protocols, ensuring regulatory compliance, and implementing preventive measures against network and information system security risks.
