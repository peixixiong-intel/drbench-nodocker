Introduction This presentation aims to provide an overview of our staff's engagement with MediConn's knowledge management platform in March 2025. We will focus on non-clinical content and company-wide updates browsing data.

Slide 3:
Overview of Knowledge Management Platform The Knowledge Management Platform was launched in 2020 to facilitate internal communication It enables seamless sharing of clinical and non-clinical information The platform is accessible to all staff members, including virtual care staff The platform is compliant with all relevant healthcare regulations It supports multiple content formats, including text, video, and audio The platform's user-friendly interface has been praised by 90% of our staff in a recent survey There were two major updates to the platform in 2024, aimed at improving the user experience

Slide 4:
Platform Usage Trends: Clinical vs Non-Clinical There has been a 30% increase in usage of the platform for clinical content since 2022 Non-clinical content usage has remained steady over the past three years In 2024, 60% of logins were for accessing clinical content The platform's usage peaked in January and July of 2024 The most accessed non-clinical content is company-wide updates The platform's user engagement rate for clinical content is consistently higher than non-clinical content

Slide 5:
Non-Clinical Content Engagement in March 2025 The most viewed non-clinical content in March 2025 was about the company's 5-year strategic plan 80% of the staff engaged with non-clinical content related to HR policies The least viewed non-clinical content was about the cafeteria menu updates The platform's average session duration for non-clinical content was 10 minutes The total number of views for non-clinical content was around 2,000 in March 2025

Slide 6:
Conclusion Understanding the platform usage trends helps us optimize the content and interface for improved engagement. As a next step, we should consider implementing strategies to encourage more frequent usage among staff.
