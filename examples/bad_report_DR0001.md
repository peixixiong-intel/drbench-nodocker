# Report on Food Safety at Lee's Market

Food safety is very important for grocery stores like Lee's Market. Regulations such
as FSMA 204 exist and companies should generally comply with them to maintain customer
trust. Being compliant is a good practice and can help the business succeed over time.

Lee's Market has been focusing on operational excellence across many parts of the
business. For example, the company's employee turnover rate averaged 12% with 150 new
hires in Q3 2024, and employees received an average of 12 hours of training with a 90%
completion rate. The company also invested in its stores: the average store renovation
cost was $250,000 across 10 stores renovated in Q2 2024. On the facilities side, total
energy consumption was 15 million kWh at a cost of $1.8M in Q1 2024.

In conclusion, Lee's Market should continue to prioritize food safety and follow the
relevant regulations in order to build trust with its customers and keep improving.
